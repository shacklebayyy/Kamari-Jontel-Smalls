Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) Certification

Trained and certified to provide direct, compassionate care to adult and geriatric residents, with hands-on experience applying that training every day at Seaside Health and Rehabilitation in Portland, Maine.

My CNA training covered the fundamentals of patient care, including safe handling techniques, infection control, vital signs, and supporting residents with activities of daily living. The program emphasized both clinical skill and compassionate, patient-centered communication.

Adult and geriatric residents rely on trained, attentive caregivers to preserve their dignity, comfort, and safety every day. Consistent, compassionate care directly supports better outcomes and stronger trust between residents, families, and the care team.

At Seaside Health and Rehabilitation, I apply this training daily by monitoring vital signs, assisting with bathing, hygiene, and mobility, and maintaining accurate clinical documentation while working closely with physicians and the nursing team to support each resident's individual care plan.

Core Competencies

  • Patient Safety Monitoring
  • Compassionate Bedside Manner
  • Accurate Vital Sign Recording
  • Activities of Daily Living Support
  • Clinical Documentation
  • Team Collaboration with Nursing Staff
