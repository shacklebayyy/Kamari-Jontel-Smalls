Patient Care & Daily Living Support

Compassionate, hands-on support for adult and geriatric residents with the activities of daily living.

Supporting Residents Every Day

As a Certified Nursing Assistant, I help residents with the daily tasks that keep them comfortable, healthy, and dignified - from bathing and grooming to feeding and mobility. Every interaction is guided by patience, respect, and attention to each resident's individual needs.

I work closely with physicians and nursing teams to carry out individualized care plans, respond promptly to patient needs, and keep accurate records so the whole care team stays informed.

Compassionate Care

Preserving patient dignity while carrying out daily care duties with warmth and respect.

Safety First

Responding to patient alarms and needs promptly to reduce risk and discomfort.

Accurate Monitoring

Recording vital signs and clinical documentation to support treatment plans.

Team Alignment

Collaborating with physicians and nursing teams to support each resident's care plan.
