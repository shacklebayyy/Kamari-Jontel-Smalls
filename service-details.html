Engineering Primary Human T Cells for Cancer Therapy

At Tmunity Therapeutics, my research centers on creating robust TCR expression platforms in primary human CD4+ and CD8+ T cells. Utilizing recombinant lentiviral vectors and targeted CRISPR/Cas9 genomic modifications, we enhance T cell specificity and longevity against solid tumors.

To maximize therapeutic efficacy, I integrate computational biology toolsets (written in Python and R) to optimize T cell expansion dynamics and analyze TCR-dextramer binding affinity. This dual wet-lab and dry-lab approach accelerates candidate selection and generates critical data supporting regulatory submissions.
